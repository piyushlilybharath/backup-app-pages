Privacy Policy

This tool is used by one company only, for the backup of that company's own files from Microsoft OneDrive to its own Google Drive account.

It accesses only the Google Drive of the account that signs in. It stores no data on any server of ours. Files are copied directly between the two cloud services by software running on the company's own computer.

No data is sold, shared with any third party or used for advertising. The sign-in keys remain on the company's own computer.
